I love Archasaurs a lot, but I'm still debating whether my favorite dinosaur is an Ankylosaurus, a Spinosaurus, or an Allosaurus. I know for sure my favorite pterosaur is a Rhamphorhynchus. Which had me thinking - people don't often know there's a difference between a Dinosaur and Pterosaur. The “dinosaurs” most people envision - while some are dinosaurs - are all Archasaurs! There are many misconceptions from movies and other media. I created this quiz and studying material to have people understand more about Archasaurs. I hope to teach you something :)
