Archasaur Information

Archasaurs include both dinosaurs and pterosaurs. Many people confuse the two, but here are some key facts:

  • Pseudosuchia: a clade including crocodile-line archosaurs.
  • Avemetatarsalia: bird-line archosaurs, including dinosaurs and pterosaurs.
  • Dinosaurs: diverse group of land-dwelling archosaurs like Ankylosaurus, Spinosaurus, Allosaurus.
  • Pterosaurs: flying archosaurs like Rhamphorhynchus and Pterodactyls.
  • Archasaurs first appeared during the Triassic period.
  • They adapted to many habitats: forests, plains, rivers, and seas.
  • Extinction events like the K-Pg mass extinction killed off many species.
