Archasaur Information

Archasaurs are a group of reptiles that include dinosaurs, pterosaurs, and other modern day animals (including birds and crocodiles).

Key Groups:

  • Pseudosuchia: Crocodiles and their extinct relatives.
  • Avemetatarsalia: Dinosaurs and birds.
  • Pterosauria: Flying reptiles.

Eras:

  • Triassic: first true dinosaurs appear.
  • Jurassic: many dinosaurs and first birds.
  • Cretacious: dinosaurs diversify, ends with mass extinction.

Habitats & Diets:

  • Herbavores: for example, Sauropods like Brachiosaurus.
  • carnivores: for example, Allosaurus, Tyrannosaurus.
  • Pterosaurs: flying predators or fish eaters.

Fun Facts:

  • Birds are modern dinosaurs!
  • Not all flying reptiles are dinosaurs.
  • Rhamphorhynchus is my favorite pterosaur.

Example Archasaurs:

  • Rhamphorhynchus: Long tailed pterosaur.
  • Ankylosaurus: Armored dinosaur with a club tail.
  • Spinosaurus: Semi aquatic dinosaur with sail.
  • Dilophosaurus: Bipedal theropod with thin crests on its head.
  • Pteranodon: Large flying Pterosaur.
  • Brachiosaurus: Tall herbivorous dinosaur.
  • Velociraptor: Small fast predator.
  • Triceratops: Three horned herbivore.
  • Dimorphodon: Early short tailed pterosaur.
